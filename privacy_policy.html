1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information required to provide you with a reliable debt tracking and shop inventory management service:

  • Account Information: When you register or log in, we collect your email address via Firebase Authentication to create and secure your personal account.
  • User-Generated Content: Debt records (debtors/creditors, names, phone numbers, items, amounts) and shop inventory data entered by you. This data is associated exclusively with your user account ID.
  • Device Information: Basic device information for app troubleshooting, crash reporting, and subscription verification.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide and improve the functionality of the App:

  • To authenticate your identity and grant access to your private debt and inventory records.
  • To sync your debt records and stock levels securely across your authorized devices.
  • To facilitate backup and restore features requested by you.
  • We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Security & Storage

We prioritize the security of your data:

  • Your data is securely stored on Google Firebase Cloud Firestore infrastructure with strict user-level authentication security rules.
  • Only you can access, edit, or delete your debt and inventory records using your account credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

Our application uses trusted third-party services provided by Google:

  • Firebase Authentication: For secure user login.
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore: For secure cloud database storage.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You retain full ownership of your data:

  • You can view, edit, or delete any debtor, creditor, or inventory record directly within the app at any time.
  • You may request complete deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the support email provided below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
